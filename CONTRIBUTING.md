# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in the RadioTools SDK.

Everyone who takes part in this project, including in issues, must follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Pull requests

We don't accept pull requests. RIVI Software maintains this repository, and we close pull requests without merging them.

## Issues

If you find a bug, a missing feature or a mistake in the docs, please [open an issue](https://github.com/rivi-software/radiotools-sdk/issues/new). Include:

- the package and its version, for example `@rivi-software/radiotools-sdk@1.2.3`
- your runtime or language version, for example Node 22, Python 3.12 or Swift 6
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
- any error output

Issues are public. Never include your `rtm_…` keys, `RADIOTOOLS_API_TOKEN`, `RADIOTOOLS_INGEST_TOKEN` or other station details you want to keep private.

Don't open an issue for a security vulnerability. Report it privately as described in the [Security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Customer support

RadioTools customers can also get help privately:

- create a ticket at [support.rivi.software](https://support.rivi.software)
- email [support@rivi.software](mailto:support@rivi.software)

Use one of these for questions about your account, your station or anything you don't want to share in a public issue.
